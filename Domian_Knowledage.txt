## Banking Domain
1. What is the banking domain?
The banking domain deals with financial services such as deposits, withdrawals, 
fund transfers, loans, payments, and account management.

2. What are the different types of bank accounts?
- Savings Account
- Current Account
- Fixed Deposit (FD)
- Recurring Deposit (RD)

4. What is the difference between NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS?
- NEFT: National Electronic fund transfer processed in batches or settlement cycles.
- RTGS: Real-time gross settlement, generally used for high-value transfers.
- IMPS: Immediate Payment Service, which supports instant fund transfers.

5. What is KYC?
KYC stands for Know Your Customer. Banks use it to verify customer identity and address to help prevent fraud and money laundering.

6. What is a transaction ID?
A transaction ID is a unique identifier assigned to a financial transaction to help track and investigate it.

11. How would you test a fund transfer functionality?
I would test valid and invalid beneficiaries, sufficient and insufficient balances, transfer limits, correct debit and credit amounts, transaction status, duplicate requests, and transaction history.

12. How would you test an ATM withdrawal?
I would test valid and invalid PINs, sufficient and insufficient balances, withdrawal limits, cash availability, account debit, receipt generation, and reversal when cash is not dispensed.

13. How would you test a login page in a banking application?
I would test valid and invalid credentials, account lockout, session timeout, MFA/OTP validation, password masking, and security against unauthorized access.
